What is Analook?

Analook is an AI-powered competitive intelligence tool designed for startup founders, indie hackers, and growth teams. Enter any product's URL and get a comprehensive analysis in about 2 minutes — covering website evolution, social media presence, SEO metrics, Product Hunt history, Google Trends peaks, and an AI-generated strategic verdict.

Unlike traditional tools that require hours of manual research, Analook automates the entire competitive analysis workflow. It aggregates data from 15+ sources and uses AI to identify growth patterns, score traffic quality, and map competitive positioning on a Strategy Radar.

What is SimilarWeb?

SimilarWeb is an established digital intelligence platform primarily known for its website traffic estimation. It uses a proprietary panel of millions of users plus ISP-level data partnerships to estimate website visits, traffic sources, and audience demographics.

SimilarWeb excels at traffic data granularity — country-level breakdowns, referral sources, and app analytics. It's the industry standard for enterprise market research teams who need detailed traffic intelligence.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Analook a good alternative to SimilarWeb?

Yes. For startup-stage competitive intelligence, Analook provides more actionable insights (AI verdicts, growth patterns, launch tracking) at 90% lower cost. SimilarWeb's strength is raw traffic data volume.

Can I use both Analook and SimilarWeb?

Absolutely. Many users start with Analook's free tier for quick competitor scans, then use SimilarWeb for deep-dive traffic analysis on specific competitors.

Does Analook have an API?

Yes. POST to /api/v1/analyze with a URL and get a full competitive intelligence report as JSON. Works with Claude Code, MCP servers, and any AI agent.
