What is Analook?

Analook is an AI-powered competitor analysis tool built for founders, growth teams, and consultants. Enter any URL and get a 60-second teardown covering 15+ signal categories — SEO/traffic, social, Product Hunt history, GitHub activity, Wayback Machine evolution, pricing, and an AI strategic verdict. Built by Iris Wei (ex-AFFiNE co-founder).

What is Owler?

Owler is a competitive intelligence platform focused on competitor news, funding events, leadership changes, and industry landscape mapping. Founded 2011 (acquired by Meltwater in 2021). Used by 5M+ business users to monitor competitor moves at scale. Owler's strength is the news feed model — you select competitors once, alerts come continuously.

Use Analook if you...

  • Need a one-shot strategic teardown for a board meeting
  • Want to research 1-5 competitors in depth per month
  • Care about strategic positioning, not just news events
  • Use AI agents (Claude, Cursor) with MCP integration
  • Need SEO + Product Hunt + GitHub signals Owler doesn't have

Use Owler if you...

  • Need ongoing alerts when competitors raise funding
  • Monitor leadership changes and key hires
  • Track competitor press coverage continuously
  • Have 10+ competitors and want a scaled news feed
  • Want competitor landscape mapping at industry level

The Complementary Workflow

  1. Owler monitors 10-20 competitors continuously
  2. Owler fires alert: "Competitor X just raised $20M Series B"
  3. Run Analook on competitor X to understand the strategic context — what did they ship recently, how is their traffic trending, what's their AI verdict
  4. Brief your team in 15 minutes with both the news + the strategic picture

Combined monthly cost: Owler Pro ($35) + Analook Pro ($19) = $64/month.
