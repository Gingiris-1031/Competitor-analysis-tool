What is Google Trends?

Google Trends is a free Google product showing search interest for any term over time, geographically. Launched in 2006, it's the canonical free tool for spotting brand-search spikes, content trends, and category interest curves. Limitations: it only shows the curve, not the cause; it doesn't integrate with any other data; it's per-search-term, not per-website.

What is Analook?

Analook is an AI-powered competitor analysis tool. Enter a URL, get a 60-second teardown covering 15+ signal categories. Google Trends is one of them — Analook integrates Google Trends data via SerpApi, then layers traffic, SEO, social, Product Hunt history, GitHub activity, pricing, Wayback evolution, and AI verdict on top. The result: a curve plus the explanation behind the curve.

Google Trends is the best free tool of its kind. Don't replace it — augment it. Use Google Trends to spot the signals; use Analook to understand them.
