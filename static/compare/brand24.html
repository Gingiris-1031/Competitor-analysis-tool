What is Brand24?

Brand24 is a social listening platform founded in 2011. Track any keyword (your brand, a competitor, an industry term) across Twitter/X, Reddit, YouTube, blogs, podcasts, and forums — get continuous mention alerts with sentiment analysis. Used by 4,000+ brands for reputation management, PR monitoring, and competitor mention tracking. Strong sentiment analysis (positive/negative/neutral classification) and influencer scoring.

What is Analook?

Analook is an AI-powered competitor analysis tool. Enter any competitor URL and get a 60-second strategic teardown covering 15+ signal categories: SEO/traffic, social media presence (detection, not mention monitoring), Product Hunt launches, GitHub activity, Wayback evolution, pricing structure, AI strategic verdict. Built for founders, growth teams, and consultants doing periodic deep competitive research.

Use Analook if you...

  • Need to research 1-5 competitors per month in strategic depth
  • Want to understand how a competitor is growing (not just what people say about them)
  • Care about SEO/traffic/Product Hunt signals Brand24 doesn't have
  • Use AI agents (Claude, Cursor) with MCP integration
  • Have a board meeting and need full competitive picture, not mention volume

Use Brand24 if you...

  • Monitor brand reputation in real time
  • Need sentiment analysis for PR/marketing decisions
  • Track 5-50 keywords continuously with alerts
  • Run influencer marketing campaigns and need ROI tracking
  • Care about who's talking about your competitors right now

Complementary Workflow (Marketing + Strategy Stack)

  1. Brand24 alerts: "Competitor X just got mentioned 500× this week with 85% positive sentiment"
  2. Run Analook on competitor X: discover they launched on Product Hunt, hit #1, got TechCrunch coverage
  3. You now know both what happened (Brand24) and why it worked (Analook)
  4. Update your team's competitive positioning

Combined: Brand24 Personal ($79) + Analook Pro ($29) = $108/month — full reputation monitoring + strategic intelligence.

Final Verdict

These two tools serve different roles in a complete marketing/strategy stack. Don't pick one to replace the other unless you have a clear single job to do.
