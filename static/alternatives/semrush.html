#2 — Ahrefs

Price: $129/month Lite

If you would buy Semrush primarily for organic-search and backlink research, Ahrefs belongs on the shortlist. Ahrefs publishes the size and freshness of its search and backlink indexes; compare those limits with the specific Semrush workflow you need. Detailed comparison: Analook vs Ahrefs.

#3 — Similarweb

Price: varies by self-service or business package

If your primary use of Semrush is traffic intelligence, Similarweb is the more specialized option. It is designed for audience size, geography, channel mix, and market benchmarking rather than a founder-oriented, multi-source teardown. Detail: 7 Similarweb alternatives.

#4 — Mangools

Price: $49/month Basic

Five focused SEO tools cover keyword research, SERP analysis, rank tracking, backlinks, and site profiling. It is a narrower suite than Semrush and can fit solo SEO and content workflows that do not need Semrush's broader campaign stack.

#5 — SE Ranking

Price: $44/month Essential

Agency-grade SEO toolkit at SMB pricing. Strong rank tracker (more cost-effective than SEMrush's), competitor analysis, on-page audit. Smaller backlink and keyword databases than SEMrush but the price gap is wide enough that most users won't notice the difference.

#6 — Ubersuggest

Price: 3 free searches/day · $29/month Individual · $290 lifetime

Aggressive free tier (3 searches/day forever) and only lifetime deal in the space. Coverage is shallower than SEMrush but for solo founders running early content SEO, it's enough. Lifetime deal at $290 is reasonable if you'll use it 2+ years.

#7 — SpyFu

Price: $39/month Basic

SpyFu focuses on competitor PPC research — historical paid keywords, ad copy, ad spend estimates. As a SEMrush alternative: weaker organic SEO data, deeper paid search history. Best if your primary use of SEMrush is "what ads are my competitors running and how much are they spending."

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best free SEMrush alternative in 2026?

The best free option depends on the job. Analook offers two complimentary multi-source competitor reports; Semrush offers limited free tools and a trial for deeper SEO workflows. Compare each option against the exact keyword, backlink, audit, traffic, or product signals you need.

Is there an Ahrefs vs SEMrush winner?

There is no universal winner. Ahrefs is commonly shortlisted for backlink and organic-search research, while Semrush combines keyword research, position tracking, site audit, backlinks, reporting, and additional marketing toolkits. Test both against the same workflow and required limits.

Why are people comparing SEMrush alternatives in 2026?

Semrush SEO Toolkit Pro is listed at $139.95 per month and is designed for recurring SEO operations. Teams with a narrower job, such as an occasional competitor teardown or traffic-only benchmarking, may prefer a focused or pay-per-use product.

Does Analook have keyword data like SEMrush?

Analook surfaces selected branded and non-branded keyword signals through third-party data providers, but it is not a replacement for Semrush's keyword research system. Semrush reports 28.4 billion keywords and is the stronger fit for deep keyword-universe exploration.

Which SEMrush alternative is best for SaaS competitive analysis?

Use Analook when the task is an occasional multi-source teardown spanning directional search and traffic signals, product launches, GitHub, social, pricing, and page history. Keep Semrush on the shortlist when deep keyword research, backlinks, rank tracking, and site audits are the primary requirements.
