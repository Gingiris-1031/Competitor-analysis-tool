7 Best SEMrush Alternatives in 2026 (Free + Paid)

SEMrush Pro is $139/month with annual lock-in. Here are 7 alternatives ranked by what you'd actually use them for — including a free option that covers more than SEMrush.

TL;DR Recommendation

  • 🥇 Best free alternative: Analook — 2 free reports/month, broader competitive signals than SEMrush
  • 🥈 Best for SEO depth: Ahrefs ($129/mo) — deepest backlinks + keyword index
  • 🥉 Best for traffic-only data: SimilarWeb ($125+/mo) — proprietary traffic panel
  • 💎 Best budget alternative: Mangools ($49/mo) — 60% of SEMrush features at 35% price

Why People Look for SEMrush Alternatives

  1. Annual commitment + $139/mo entry. Monthly billing exists but with a 16% surcharge. Either way, $1,400-$1,668/year is a serious commitment for ad-hoc research.
  2. Marketing-team scope. SEMrush is built for in-house marketing teams managing SEO + paid + content + social. For SaaS founders doing competitive research on product strategy (not marketing campaigns), it's overkill.
  3. Underused features. SEMrush's 50+ tools are intimidating; most users use 5-10. The unused 40+ are paid for but never opened.

#1 — Analook

Best for Founders

Price: Free for 2 reports/month · $29/month Pro · $5 single report

Full disclosure: I built Analook. SEMrush has 50+ marketing tools. Analook does one thing: give you a full competitive teardown of any URL in 60 seconds. The catch is that "competitive teardown" includes SEO + traffic + social + Product Hunt + GitHub + pricing + AI verdict — closer to SEMrush's breadth than SEMrush's depth in any single dimension.

vs SEMrush specifically: SEMrush is built for ongoing campaign management. Analook is built for one-shot strategic teardowns. If you run weekly SEO sprints, SEMrush wins. If you research 1-5 competitors per month, Analook wins on cost and breadth-of-signal.

Best for: SaaS founders, growth consultants, anyone using Claude/Cursor (Analook exposes an MCP server). Try free at analook.com. Detailed head-to-head: Analook vs SEMrush.

🥈

#2 — Ahrefs

Price: $129/month Lite

If you'd buy SEMrush primarily for SEO depth, Ahrefs is the better choice — larger backlink index (35T+), faster SERP data refresh, cleaner keyword research interface. Trade-off: less marketing breadth (no paid search, weaker social). Detailed comparison: Analook vs Ahrefs.

🥉

#3 — SimilarWeb

Price: $125+/month Business

If your primary use of SEMrush is Traffic Analytics, SimilarWeb's proprietary panel data is more accurate for established websites (>10K monthly visits). For early-stage competitor research, SimilarWeb often returns "insufficient data". Detail: 7 SimilarWeb alternatives.

#4 — Mangools

Price: $49/month Basic

Five SEO tools bundled at solo-founder pricing (KWFinder, SERPChecker, SERPWatcher, LinkMiner, SiteProfiler). Excellent UX, especially for keyword difficulty visualization. As a SEMrush alternative: ~50% of feature breadth at ~35% of the price. Best for solo SEO + content creators.

#5 — SE Ranking

Price: $44/month Essential

Agency-grade SEO toolkit at SMB pricing. Strong rank tracker (more cost-effective than SEMrush's), competitor analysis, on-page audit. Smaller backlink and keyword databases than SEMrush but the price gap is wide enough that most users won't notice the difference.

#6 — Ubersuggest

Price: 3 free searches/day · $29/month Individual · $290 lifetime

Aggressive free tier (3 searches/day forever) and only lifetime deal in the space. Coverage is shallower than SEMrush but for solo founders running early content SEO, it's enough. Lifetime deal at $290 is reasonable if you'll use it 2+ years.

#7 — SpyFu

Price: $39/month Basic

SpyFu focuses on competitor PPC research — historical paid keywords, ad copy, ad spend estimates. As a SEMrush alternative: weaker organic SEO data, deeper paid search history. Best if your primary use of SEMrush is "what ads are my competitors running and how much are they spending."
