SimilarWeb's Business plan starts at $125/month with annual commitment. Here are 7 alternatives — ranked by what you'd actually use them for, not by who has the biggest marketing budget.

  • 🥇 Best free alternative: Analook — 2 free reports/month, covers traffic + 14 other signal categories
  • 🥈 Best for deep SEO: Ahrefs ($129/mo) — deepest backlink data

Why People Look for SimilarWeb Alternatives

Three patterns come up most often:

  1. Price — SimilarWeb Business starts at $125/month with annual commitment. For founders doing 2-3 competitor checks per month, that's overkill.
  2. Coverage gap on early-stage websites — SimilarWeb often returns "insufficient data" for competitors under ~10K monthly visits. If you're researching early-stage SaaS, you need a different approach.
  3. Single dimension — SimilarWeb is excellent at traffic, weak on everything else (social, Product Hunt, GitHub, AI verdict, pricing-page evolution). Modern competitor research is multi-source.

If any of those resonate, one of the 7 alternatives below probably fits better than SimilarWeb itself.

#1 — Analook

Best Free Alternative

Price: Free for 2 reports/month · $19/month Pro · $5 single report

Full disclosure: I built Analook. It exists because I needed a tool that gave me the full picture of a competitor in 60 seconds — not just traffic data. Enter any URL and Analook fetches 15+ data sources (Wayback evolution, SEO via DataForSEO, social, Product Hunt history, GitHub, pricing, Google Trends peaks) and runs an AI strategic verdict.

vs SimilarWeb specifically: Analook's traffic data is directional rather than panel-based — less accurate for huge enterprise sites, more useful for early-stage competitors that SimilarWeb returns "insufficient data" on. Where Analook clearly wins: Product Hunt tracking, GitHub stars analysis, Wayback Machine evolution, and AI verdict — none of which SimilarWeb has.

Use case: early-stage SaaS, founders doing 1-5 competitor teardowns per month, growth consultants, anyone who uses Claude/Cursor (Analook exposes a remote MCP server). Try it free at analook.com.

🥈

#2 — Ahrefs

Best for SEO Depth

Price: $129/month Lite

Ahrefs has the largest backlink index on the planet (35T+ links) and the deepest organic keyword data. If your acquisition is >50% SEO-driven and you're trying to outrank specific competitors on specific keywords, Ahrefs is genuinely irreplaceable. As a SimilarWeb alternative: similar price, deeper SEO data, weaker traffic estimation. Detail: see Analook vs Ahrefs.

🥉

#3 — SEMrush

Broadest Marketing Suite

Price: $139/month Pro

SEMrush is the broadest marketing analytics suite — SEO, paid search, social, content, and traffic analytics all under one subscription. The Traffic Analytics module specifically is the closest direct SimilarWeb replacement, with the bonus that you also get keyword and backlink data in the same dashboard. Detail: see Analook vs SEMrush.

#4 — Sparktoro

Price: 3 free searches/month · $50/month Personal

Sparktoro tackles a different question entirely: not "how much traffic does my competitor get" but "who is my competitor's audience and what else do they pay attention to?" It surfaces which podcasts, YouTube channels, websites, and Twitter accounts a defined audience overlaps with. For positioning and audience research, Sparktoro is uniquely useful — not really a SimilarWeb replacement, but often what people actually need when they search for one.

#5 — SE Ranking

Price: $44/month Essential

Budget alternative to Ahrefs/SEMrush. Covers rank tracking, competitor analysis, backlink checker, and on-page audit. Smaller backlink index than Ahrefs but a third of the price. For teams that want a single subscription covering SEO basics + competitor traffic estimates, SE Ranking is the most cost-effective option in this list (after free tools).

#6 — Cloudflare Radar

Price: Free

Cloudflare Radar uses Cloudflare's traffic data (which sees ~20% of global web traffic) to surface trending domains, traffic patterns, and outage data. For directional traffic insights on large sites, it's a useful free signal. Limitations: no per-channel breakdown, no keyword data, no competitive overlap analysis. Treat it as "free traffic trend monitor," not a SimilarWeb replacement.

#7 — Google Trends + Wayback Machine

Price: Free

The DIY stack: Google Trends shows brand-search interest over time (great for catching launch peaks), Wayback Machine shows how a competitor's positioning evolved month by month, and Google's autocomplete reveals what users associate with their brand. Combined, these three free tools give you most of what SimilarWeb provides, minus the dashboards. Trade-off: 2-3 hours per competitor manually vs 60 seconds with Analook.
