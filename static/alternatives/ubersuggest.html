Why People Look for Ubersuggest Alternatives

  1. Free tier collapse. Ubersuggest's free tier dropped from "unlimited 3 free searches" to a hard 3-searches-per-day cap with watered-down data behind the paywall.
  2. Lifetime deal discontinued. The $290 lifetime deal that anchored Ubersuggest's reputation ended in late 2025. Subscription-only now.
  3. Data quality concerns. Keyword volume estimates often diverge meaningfully from Ahrefs/Semrush. Fine for directional research, frustrating for client deliverables.

#2 — Mangools

Price: $29/month Mangools Basic · 10-day trial

Five SEO tools bundled (KWFinder, SERPChecker, SERPWatcher, LinkMiner, SiteProfiler). Closest direct Ubersuggest competitor at similar price — keyword research with cleaner UX and arguably more accurate volume estimates.

#3 — Ahrefs Webmaster Tools (AWT)

Price: Free (your verified sites only)

Ahrefs' free tier for site owners — gives you keyword and backlink data for properties you verify in Google Search Console. Not a direct Ubersuggest replacement for researching competitors, but free Ahrefs-quality data for your own site.

Best for: Solo founders who only need to monitor their own site's SEO. Anyone else: paid Ahrefs at $129/mo. See: 7 Ahrefs alternatives.

#4 — KeySearch

Price: $17/month Starter · 1-month free trial

Cheaper than Ubersuggest paid tier with comparable feature set — keyword research, competitive analysis, rank tracking. Smaller keyword database but better signal-to-noise for blogger / affiliate workflows.

Best for: Solo bloggers + niche site builders. Most underrated paid SEO tool.

#5 — SE Ranking

Price: $44/month Essential · 14-day trial

Step up from Ubersuggest in feature depth and data accuracy. Strong rank tracker, full SEO toolkit including white-label reports. Best for users who outgrew Ubersuggest but can't justify Ahrefs/Semrush pricing.

Best for: Agency SEO or anyone who outgrew Ubersuggest's depth.

#6 — Semrush

Price: $139.95/month Pro

Step up to professional tier. Massive feature set (50+ tools), 25B+ keyword database. Overkill if you only need keyword research, mandatory if you need full marketing-team workflows.

Best for: In-house marketing teams. See: 7 SEMrush alternatives.

#7 — Google Keyword Planner

Price: Free (with Google Ads account)

Google's own keyword planner — most accurate volume estimates available (it's Google's own data). Volumes are bucketed, not exact, but trustworthy. Requires a Google Ads account; you don't need to actively run ads to access it.

Best for: Final volume verification. Use alongside Mangools/Keysearch for ideas + GKP for accuracy.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Ubersuggest still worth using in 2026?

For absolute beginners with no SEO experience and zero budget — yes, the free tier (3 searches/day) is functional for casual keyword exploration. For anyone doing meaningful SEO work, Mangools ($29) or KeySearch ($17) give better data at comparable or lower prices.

Why did Ubersuggest end the lifetime deal?

The $290 lifetime deal was an acquisition play that worked too well — too many lifetime customers with no recurring revenue. Most lifetime-deal businesses (AppSumo bundles excluded) sunset the deal within 2-3 years. Ubersuggest lasted longer than most.

Is Mangools better than Ubersuggest?

For most users, yes. Mangools' KWFinder has more accurate volume estimates, the difficulty score correlates better with actual rankings, and the UI is meaningfully cleaner. Same $29 price tier.

What is the best free Ubersuggest alternative in 2026?

Analook (free 2 reports/month) for broader competitive analysis. Google Keyword Planner (free with Google Ads account) for accurate volume data. Ahrefs Webmaster Tools (free) if you only need data for your own verified site.

Can I use Google Keyword Planner without running ads?

Yes. You need an active Google Ads account (free to create), but you don't need to spend money or run any campaigns. Note: without active spend, volume estimates are bucketed into ranges (e.g., 10K-100K) rather than exact numbers.
