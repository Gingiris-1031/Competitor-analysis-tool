#3 — Cloudflare Radar

Price: Free (no signup)

Cloudflare's public Radar dashboard exposes its 20% of internet traffic data — domain ranking by category, popularity, and country. Closest free-tier equivalent to old Alexa Rank, with much fresher data (daily updates).

Best for: Free rank checking. Limitations: no per-page traffic data, no keyword info.

#4 — Semrush

Price: $139.95/month Pro

Includes Traffic Analytics module (formerly the "SEMrush.Trends" product) which replaced Alexa for many SEO teams. Comprehensive but expensive — overkill if you only want a rank number.

Best for: SEO teams who already pay for SEMrush; Traffic Analytics is included. See: 7 SEMrush alternatives.

#5 — Statcounter Global Stats

Price: Free

Aggregated browser, OS, and search-engine market share stats — public dataset. Not a direct Alexa replacement for per-site rank, but useful for the macro-trend questions Alexa used to answer ("is Chrome still gaining share?").

Best for: Macro market-share research, not per-site competitor analysis.

#6 — SimilarSites

Price: Free

Discovery tool — give it a URL, get sites that are "similar" by topic and audience overlap. Solves the "who else is in this space" question Alexa's Related Sites used to answer.

Best for: Discovering competitors. Not for traffic estimation.

#7 — Ahrefs

Price: $129/month Lite

Ahrefs Rank (DR/UR) is a backlinks-based authority signal — closer in spirit to Alexa Rank than to Google PageRank. Free Webmaster Tools tier gives your own site's DR.

Best for: SEO depth + backlinks-based authority. See: 7 Ahrefs alternatives.

Why Replacing Alexa Is Harder Than It Sounds

Most people searching for an Alexa alternative are actually looking for three different things — and conflating them slows them down.

Use case 1: "I want to know if my competitor is bigger than me"

This is a competitive sanity check. Alexa Rank was a shortcut for this. The real answer isn't one number — it's traffic estimate + SEO visibility + social reach together. Analook gives all three in one 60-second audit, free.

Use case 2: "I need traffic data for an ad pitch or investor deck"

This requires a panel-based traffic estimate with a methodology you can cite. SimilarWeb is the right answer here — more expensive, but the only credible source for professional-grade traffic numbers.

Use case 3: "I want to understand what a competitor is doing well — and why"

This is competitive intelligence, not ranking. Alexa never answered this. Analook does: SEO keyword gaps, growth trajectory, Product Hunt launch history, GitHub momentum, social presence, and an AI-generated verdict on the strategy — in 3 minutes.

Frequently Asked Questions

When did Alexa.com shut down?

Amazon retired Alexa Internet (alexa.com) on May 1, 2026. The decision was announced in December 2025. The Alexa Rank methodology had been frozen since the early 2010s, making the closure long-expected by industry observers.

What is the best free Alexa alternative in 2026?

Analook offers 2 free reports/month with a full competitive teardown (traffic, SEO, social, GitHub, Product Hunt, AI verdict) — the broadest free alternative. Cloudflare Radar offers free domain ranking with no signup but no per-page data.

Why was Alexa Rank inaccurate?

Alexa's methodology relied on a browser toolbar panel. By 2020, the panel was self-selected (mostly Western, mostly SEO professionals) and increasingly small relative to global internet traffic. Estimates for sites outside the top 100K were essentially noise.

Did Amazon migrate Alexa data to another tool?

No. Amazon did not migrate the alexa.com data to any other product. Amazon Brand Analytics serves a different use case (Amazon-marketplace intent data, not general web ranking).

Can I still get Alexa Rank for a site?

Not officially. Some Chrome extensions and third-party caches show stale rank data from late 2025. For current accurate ranking, use SimilarWeb's category rank, Cloudflare Radar's domain rank, or Analook's traffic-estimate signal.
