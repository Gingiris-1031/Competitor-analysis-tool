Why People Look for Moz Alternatives

  1. Price vs. output. Moz Pro Starter is $99/month — meaningfully above what early-stage SaaS teams want to spend on a tool that's essentially one metric (DA) wrapped in SEO features that Ahrefs and SEMrush do better.
  2. Dated feature set. Moz pioneered modern SEO and the DA score remains the industry's shared language. But in 2026, its keyword explorer, rank tracker, and site audit are competitive with tools that cost 35–50% less. The brand carries more weight than the current product.
  3. SEO-only scope. Moz has zero coverage of the competitive signals that matter most to product teams: how a competitor launched on Product Hunt, when their GitHub stars spiked, what their pricing looked like 18 months ago. If your competitive question goes beyond backlinks and keywords, Moz won't answer it.

#1 — Analook

Best for SaaS Founders

Price: Free — 2 reports/month · Pro $19/month (30 reports) · Single report $5

Full disclosure: I built Analook. Moz's value proposition is domain authority and SEO fundamentals. Analook's value proposition is answering "how is this competitor actually growing" — which is a different question that requires 15+ signal categories beyond SEO. In 60 seconds, Analook surfaces SEO traffic estimates, top backlinks, social media presence, Product Hunt launch history, GitHub open-source activity, pricing structure, Wayback Machine website evolution, PR mentions, and an AI-generated strategic verdict.

Where Analook wins vs Moz: Everything outside the SEO silo. Moz has zero data on Product Hunt (did the competitor launch? how many upvotes? was it a #1 product of the day?), GitHub star growth, Twitter/X follower trajectory, or pricing page history. These signals matter if you're a SaaS founder benchmarking growth strategy, not just SEO rankings.

Where Moz still wins: Domain Authority is the reporting standard. If your SEO team's KPIs or client reports use DA as a benchmark metric, you need Moz — nobody else's score will substitute without stakeholder buy-in. Also: Moz's link explorer and on-page grader are more polished for pure SEO workflows.

Best for: SaaS founders, growth consultants, and anyone using Claude Desktop or Cursor (Analook has a remote MCP server). Try 2 free reports at analook.com — no credit card.

🥈

#2 — Ahrefs

Strongest Backlink Database

Price: $129/month Lite · $249/month Standard

If you're evaluating Moz because of its backlink capabilities, Ahrefs is the real upgrade. The gap is significant: Ahrefs indexes 35T+ backlinks vs Moz's smaller crawler, refreshes data faster, and has a far more capable keyword explorer. Domain Rating (DR) is Ahrefs' equivalent of DA — it correlates highly and is widely accepted among SEO practitioners. The price premium over Moz ($30/month more) is hard to argue against given the capability delta. See: 7 Best Ahrefs Alternatives.

🥉

#3 — SEMrush

SEO + Content + Ads in One

Price: $139/month Pro

SEMrush is the broadest marketing analytics suite — SEO, content strategy, PPC competitor research, social scheduling, and traffic analytics under one roof. As a Moz alternative: it does everything Moz does plus paid search and content auditing, at $40/month more. If your team is currently opening both Moz and another tool for PPC or content data, SEMrush collapses the stack. See: 7 Best SEMrush Alternatives.

#4 — SE Ranking

Price: $65/month Essential · $119/month Pro

The cleanest Moz feature swap at lower cost. SE Ranking covers rank tracking, backlink checker, keyword research, competitor analysis, and on-page audit — essentially every feature that drives Moz Pro's $99/month price tag — at $65/month. The Domain Trust score correlates well with Moz DA. The backlink index is smaller than Ahrefs but comparable to Moz's. Best for: SEO teams or agencies who use Moz for its core feature set and want to trim the bill by 35% without retraining workflows.

#5 — Mangools

Price: $49/month Basic · $69/month Premium

Five SEO tools bundled at half Moz's price: KWFinder (keyword research with the best UX in the segment), SERPChecker (SERP analysis), SERPWatcher (rank tracking), LinkMiner (backlink explorer), and SiteProfiler (domain overview). Mangools trades index depth for UX quality — the keyword difficulty visualization and SERP result breakdown are best-in-class at this price point. Ideal for: solo SEO professionals and content strategists who value a clean, fast interface over raw data volume.

#6 — Ubersuggest

Price: Free (3 searches/day) · $29/month Individual · $290 one-time lifetime

Neil Patel's SEO tool with the most generous free tier in this comparison and the only lifetime purchase option. Feature depth is shallower than Moz — smaller keyword universe, less granular backlink data — but for individual bloggers, content creators, and indie founders running basic keyword research and traffic analysis, Ubersuggest at $29/month is genuinely sufficient. The $290 lifetime deal is the best value in the SEO tool market if you'll use it longer than 10 months.

#7 — Majestic

Price: $50/month Lite · $100/month Pro

Majestic is the specialist's choice for backlink analysis. Its Trust Flow (TF) and Citation Flow (CF) metrics are distinct from DA and provide a different angle on link quality — TF measures topical relevance of linking sites, which many link-building professionals consider more actionable than raw DA scores. Majestic has no keyword research, no rank tracking, no on-page audit. It does one thing (backlinks) with depth and a unique metric framework. Best for: dedicated link-building specialists who prioritize TF/CF alongside or instead of DA.

When to Use Analook Instead of Moz

"I need to know how my competitor is growing, not just their domain authority"

→ Analook. Product Hunt launches, GitHub star spikes, social media trajectory, pricing changes, website evolution via Wayback Machine — none of which Moz covers. Full competitor teardown in 60 seconds, $5 pay-per-use or free tier.

"I need DA scores for external link audits or KPI reporting to stakeholders"

→ Moz is still the standard here. DA is the metric non-SEO stakeholders actually recognize. No alternative score carries the same shared vocabulary. Keep Moz for DA-dependent workflows.

"$99/month is too much — I just need basic keyword research and rank tracking"

→ Ubersuggest at $29/month or SE Ranking at $65/month. Both cover Moz's core use cases at lower cost.

"I want deep backlink intelligence — Moz's index feels thin"

→ Ahrefs ($129/mo) for the largest backlink index, or Majestic ($50/mo) for TF/CF-focused link analysis without paying Ahrefs prices.
