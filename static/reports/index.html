Frequently Asked Questions

What is in an Analook competitor intelligence report?

Each report covers: organic search traffic estimate (via keyword rankings), total ranked keywords, Product Hunt launch history, GitHub organization stats, social media presence, pricing model, press and HackerNews coverage, and an AI-generated growth strategy analysis. How accurate are the traffic estimates?

Traffic figures are organic search estimates based on keyword ranking data from DataForSEO — they reflect estimated organic search visits driven by keyword positions, not total website traffic. Actual total traffic is typically significantly higher. All figures in these reports are labeled as "organic search estimate via keyword rankings."
